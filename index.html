Safety disclaimer

MediSafe is a decision-support platform, not a replacement for doctors or pharmacists. AI predictions are estimates, not confirmed medical facts. An unknown interaction does not mean a combination is safe, and the lowest price is not automatically the best choice. Never start, stop or replace a prescribed medicine without professional advice.
